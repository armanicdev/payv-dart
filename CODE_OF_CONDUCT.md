# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about code, not about people.

## What that means here

This project is about getting text right for languages that most software gets
wrong, and the people who care about that come from everywhere. Everyone is
welcome regardless of nationality, ethnicity, language, religion, gender,
sexuality, disability, age or experience level.

Expected:

- Criticise the work, not the person. "This drops a letter in `fin2`" is useful;
  "you clearly don't understand OpenType" is not.
- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith until the code
  says otherwise.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no. Scope is a real constraint, not a slight.
- Write down what you measured. A claim with evidence ends an argument; a claim
  without one starts a long thread.

Not accepted:

- Harassment, slurs, or demeaning comments about any of the categories above.
- Sustained disruption, personal attacks, or dragging unrelated political
  fights into issues and PRs.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or imagery.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, code comments, and any
space where someone is representing this project.

## Reporting

Email the maintainer at **armanhema12@gmail.com**. Reports stay private. You
will get a reply, and you will not be asked to justify being uncomfortable.

Consequences run from a request to stop, through a warning, to being blocked
from the repository. Which one depends on severity and on whether it happens
again — not on how valuable someone's contributions are.
